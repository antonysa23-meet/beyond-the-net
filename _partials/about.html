Our Mission

At Beyond the Net, our mission is to mentor underserved youth in post-secondary education and character development, with volleyball as our medium. We hope to impact their lives through education and fun, with an emphasis on students from immigrant, refugee, minority, limited-income, and first-generation backgrouds.

Our Vision

Our vision is to increase the number of underserved and first-generation youth that study at world-class institutions, develop them as future leaders in their communities, and spark a love for learning and playing volleyball.
