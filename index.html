Welcome to Beyond the Net.

Empowering youth with mentorship that goes farther than the court.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to mentoring underserved youth in post-secondary education, healthy lifestyles, and character development - with volleyball as our medium.

Get to Know Us

Our Impact

70 students mentored in two states.
250 families reached across the country.

Our Services

From one-on-one college application help to providing volunteer hour opportunities, we offer a range of opportunities for students to develop as leaders in their community.

Supporters & Collaborators

Our dedicated partners and sponsors play a vital role in helping us make a positive impact on the lives of young individuals. Join us in our mission to empower the next generation.
