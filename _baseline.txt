About Beyond the Net
Learn more about Beyond the Net's vision, mission statement, and founders and executive directors.
Our Mission
At Beyond the Net, our mission is to mentor underserved youth in post-secondary education and character development, with volleyball as our medium. We hope to impact their lives through education and fun, with an emphasis on students from immigrant, refugee, minority, limited-income, and first-generation backgrouds.
Our Vision
Our vision is to increase the number of underserved and first-generation youth that study at world-class institutions, develop them as future leaders in their communities, and spark a love for learning and playing volleyball.

Our Programs
Learn more about how we impact Houston youth - on and off the court.
Our Services
Our community service is designed to help propel underserved youth in the Greater Houston area and develop them as scholars and future leaders. We have a particular focus on minority, immigrant, and refugee youth and hope to bridge the gap in immigrant students and university-level education.
How We Help
Below is a delineation of how we engage with our community's future generation.
College Application Mentorship
We provide individualized college application help for every student and work longitudinally with them to create the best narrative and application - free of charge.
Character Development
Team sports improve communication and teamwork skills, while helping players create long-lasting bonds with their teammates. We foster character development with a growth mindset, ensuring everyone learns something each week.
Leadership Skills
In the second half of the semester, high school students will lead some of their own sessions, mentoring elementary and middle school students - developing them as leaders and creating connections within the community's youth.
Volunteer Hours
High school mentors will gain volunteer hours - as a recognition of their hard work - which will strengthen their college applications. They develop as servant leaders of their community and demonstrate what it means to be kind.
Healthy Living
Every student needs an outlet for their responsibilities and stress, whether it be within the home or school. Volleyball provides a way to destress and exercise, while educating program participants about living healthy lives through diet and exercise.
Health Education
Financially-burdened or immigrant families may struggle in navigating health insurance eligibility, federal benefits, and other social safety net programs. We provide information to close this gap in understanding and access to healthcare resources.
